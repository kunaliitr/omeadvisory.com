About OME

Where policy, methodology and implementation meet.

OME Advisory is an independent climate, carbon and sustainability advisory founded by Kunal Sharma in Germany and working internationally.

Perspective

OME's approach is shaped by experience across international climate mechanisms, corporate decarbonisation, industrial sustainability, ESG methodologies and sustainability data. That combination helps bridge the gap between what frameworks require and what organisations can practically implement.

Our work is evidence-led and outcome-focused: establish the facts, identify what matters, design a credible pathway and prepare the organisation for scrutiny.
